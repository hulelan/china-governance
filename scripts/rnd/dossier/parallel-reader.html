Sources. Every Chinese text on this page is reproduced from the project corpus, which holds it as crawled from the issuing body or an official gazette. The document identifier beside each one (id, site) is its row in that corpus, so any figure here can be traced back to a specific stored document. These are official documents of PRC state organs. Press coverage of the notice is linked, never reproduced.

Translation. The English is an original working translation prepared for this reader, not an official or authoritative one, and no existing translation was used as a source.
