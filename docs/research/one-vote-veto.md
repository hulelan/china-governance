# The one-vote veto's life-cycle: from family planning to safety, and then out

*Findings memo, 2026-10-09. NBER w33741 (Li, Meng, Miller, Yang, 2025) argues that the One Child
Policy bit because of **一票否决** — the "one-vote veto", a rule barring a cadre's promotion
outright for missing a target — and studies its effect inside that one policy. The document record
can ask the complementary question the paper does not: **which targets carry a veto, from when, and
at what level.** The answer is a complete institutional life-cycle, and the paper's own domain is the
one that exits. Companion to `attention-campaigns.md` (campaign-style governance) and
`local-legislative-devolution.md` (a named device with a datable documentary footprint).*

---

## The short version

**1,019 documents** mention 一票否决. Across five periods the device goes
**96 → 235 → 237 → 278 → 169**, peaking in 2018-22 and falling by 39% since.

**Family planning, the paper's domain, goes from the plurality to exactly zero.** Of the veto
documents in each period, the share also naming 计划生育 runs **40.6% → 17.9% → 21.1% → 7.6% →
0.0%** — tracking the policy's own end (two-child 2016, three-child 2021). The paper measures the
device at its origin; the record shows it leaving.

**It migrated to workplace safety and the environment, then those receded too.** 安全生产 held
30-35% of veto documents for three consecutive periods before falling to 10.7%; 环境保护 peaked at
39.6% in 2008-12 and is now 8.3%. Meanwhile **poverty alleviation and food security entered**:
脱贫攻坚 0.4% → **8.3%** (2018-22), 粮食安全 1.7% → **6.5%**, 耕地保护 1.0% → 4.7%.

**And the level profile inverts.** The veto begins as a **local** device (municipal **67%**
pre-2008), becomes centrally articulated (central **31%** in 2018-22), and ends up provincial and
commentarial (provincial 31%, media 28% in 2023-26).

**What the record does NOT explain is why it retreated** — see §4, which is the most useful part of
this memo.

Shares exceed 100% because a document may name several domains — the table is co-occurrence, not a
partition.

Two shapes worth separating. **计划生育 and 社会稳定 decline monotonically** from a pre-2008 peak:
these are the device's founding domains, and both leave. **节能减排 and 食品安全 spike and vanish**
(34.9% in 2008-12, 19.8% in 2013-17) — those are campaign attachments, matching
`attention-campaigns.md`'s punctuated pattern. **脱贫攻坚, 粮食安全, 耕地保护 enter late** and are
the only domains whose share is higher now than ever.

## 2. The level inversion

| period | documents | profile |
|---|---|---|
| pre-2008 | 96 | municipal **67%**, provincial 32% |
| 2008-12 | 235 | municipal 50%, provincial 29%, central 11% |
| 2013-17 | 237 | municipal 41%, provincial 26%, central 24% |
| 2018-22 | 278 | **central 31%**, municipal 27%, provincial 24% |
| 2023-26 | 169 | provincial 31%, **media 28%**, municipal 16%, central 14% |

**Local practice, central codification, retreat.** The veto is a municipal instrument before the
center writes much about it, and the central share quadruples (11% → 31%) exactly as the overall
volume peaks. That ordering matters: it is the same local-first shape as
`patient-capital-cascade.md`, in a very different policy area, and it is visible only because
`doc_identity` gives a per-document level (`corpus-lessons.md` A1).

*(The 2023-26 row's **media 28%** is a caution, not a finding: the corpus holds ~98k 2026 documents
against ~29k for 2024, so the latest period is media-weighted by composition. Read the level
**ordering** across periods, not the 2023-26 levels.)*

## 3. What this adds to w33741

The paper establishes that the veto **worked** — enforcement, not content, made the One Child Policy
bite. It cannot see what the device did next, because its outcome data is that one policy. The
document record supplies the life-cycle: the veto was **borrowed** from family planning into safety,
environment and energy, **codified centrally** around 2018-22, **attached to campaigns** that then
ended, and is now **retreating from almost every domain it ever held**. A promotion-blocking rule is
not a fixed feature of the incentive system; it is an instrument that circulates, and the circulation
is datable.

## 4. The retreat is unexplained, and the obvious explanation fails

The tempting story is the **基层减负** ("reduce the grassroots burden") campaign against
形式主义 and excessive accountability. **Our record does not support it.**

- `基层减负` appears in **954** documents, but only **25 of them (2.6%)** also mention 一票否决. They
  are overwhelmingly about 形式主义 (**69.2%**), with 督查检查考核 5.9% and 层层加码 4.1%.
- **`滥用一票否决` returns zero**, and zero by LIKE as well — **no document in the corpus frames the
  veto as abused.** (Checked with `fts.diagnose_zero`, because a bare FTS zero is not evidence of
  absence; here it is.)
- The two series are not even synchronised. 基层减负 runs 1 (2015) · 2 (2018) · 16 · 42 · 46 · 45 ·
  37 · 106 · 246 · **403 (2026)** — it **grows hardest after** the veto has already declined.

So the 基层减负 explanation fails. Of the three remaining candidates — a central instruction we do
not hold (plausibly internal); the device following its domains out; or the promotion system
**substituting** other instruments — the third was checkable, and §5 checks it.

## 5. There was substitution, and then a general retreat

Measured on a **fixed 17-site panel** (sites with ≥40 bodied documents in every year 2012-2024),
which the periodisation in §1 lacks and which matters because the raw series is composition-driven.
Rates per **1,000 panel documents**:

| instrument | 2008-12 | 2013-17 | 2018-22 | 2023-25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **一票否决** (veto) | 12.36 | 9.97 | 5.35 | **1.96** |
| 目标责任 (target responsibility) | **47.62** | 32.60 | 13.43 | **5.24** |
| 挂牌督办 (listed supervision) | 14.23 | 12.97 | 8.77 | 4.74 |
| 终身追责 (lifetime accountability) | 0.00 | 0.94 | 0.69 | 0.60 |
| **约谈** (regulatory talks) | 7.97 | **30.97** | **33.47** | 18.71 |
| 问责 (accountability) | 19.68 | **38.27** | 34.46 | 26.61 |
| 绩效考核 (performance appraisal) | 33.39 | **51.18** | 43.84 | 20.29 |
| *panel documents* | *17,073* | *19,050* | *33,281* | *18,336* |

**First, the veto's decline is real.** On a fixed panel it falls **12.36 → 1.96**, an **84%**
decline, so §1's retreat is not a composition artifact. The same is true of 目标责任 (−89%).

**Second, there was a substitution window, 2013-22.** `约谈` **quadruples** (7.97 → 30.97) in exactly
the period the veto begins falling, and holds at 33.47 through 2018-22. 问责 nearly doubles
(19.68 → 38.27) and 绩效考核 peaks at 51.18. So the **hard, promotion-blocking** devices (一票否决,
目标责任) give way to **softer, conversational and procedural** ones (约谈 — being summoned for a
talk — and 问责 / 绩效考核). That is a change in the *form* of accountability, not its disappearance,
and it is the answer to §4's third candidate: **yes, substitution happened.**

**Third, and this is what the substitution story cannot cover: 2023-25 is a general retreat.** Every
instrument in the table is below its own peak, including the substitutes — 约谈 halves (33.47 →
18.71), 绩效考核 falls 54%, 问责 falls 23%. So the veto's *latest* decline is **not** substitution;
it sits inside a broad contraction of the whole accountability vocabulary on a fixed panel.

**What remains unexplained is therefore narrower and sharper than before.** Not "why did the veto
retreat" but **"why did the entire promotion-pressure vocabulary contract after 2022"** — with
基层减负 ruled out as the documentary driver (§4) even though its own usage grows in that window.
§6 answers it, on the same panel.

## 6. The register rotated: from pressure and procedure to aspiration

Same fixed 17-site panel, same per-1,000 basis. Change from 2018-22 to 2023-25:

| vocabulary | 2008-12 | 2013-17 | 2018-22 | 2023-25 | change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **accountability** | | | | | |
| 一票否决 | 12.36 | 9.97 | 5.35 | 1.96 | **−63%** |
| 目标责任 | 47.62 | 32.60 | 13.43 | 5.24 | **−61%** |
| 绩效考核 | 33.39 | 51.18 | 43.84 | 20.29 | −54% |
| 挂牌督办 | 14.23 | 12.97 | 8.77 | 4.74 | −46% |
| 约谈 | 7.97 | 30.97 | 33.47 | 18.71 | −44% |
| 问责 | 19.68 | 38.27 | 34.46 | 26.61 | −23% |
| **procedural reform** | | | | | |
| 简政放权 | 4.92 | 51.34 | 17.22 | 1.75 | **−90%** |
| 放管服 | 0.00 | 12.60 | 67.61 | 11.45 | **−83%** |
| 一网通办 | 0.00 | 1.94 | 25.06 | 14.07 | −44% |
| 政务服务 | 9.02 | 34.65 | 82.36 | 65.72 | −20% |
| **developmental** | | | | | |
| **高质量发展** | 0.29 | 0.94 | 182.99 | **384.93** | **+110%** |
| **新质生产力** | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | **64.35** | **new** |
| 数字化转型 | 0.70 | 0.37 | 15.02 | 32.94 | +119% |
| 免申即享 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 3.79 | 6.22 | +64% |
| 营商环境 | 4.16 | 26.19 | 93.24 | 102.15 | +10% |

**It is not accountability giving way to service delivery.** The procedural-reform vocabulary falls
as hard as the accountability one, and harder in places — 简政放权 **−90%**, 放管服 **−83%**. Both
the language of **pressure** and the language of **procedure** recede together.

**What occupies the space is developmental goal language.** 高质量发展 reaches **384.93 per 1,000**,
i.e. **38.5% of every document on the panel**, and 新质生产力 goes from **exactly zero** to 64.35 in
a single period. That is the rotation: from *how cadres will be held to targets* and *how procedures
will be simplified*, to *what the economy is becoming*.

***A caveat that is also a finding.*** *A phrase in 38.5% of all documents is not a policy signal,
it is a **register** — closer to a letterhead than to an instruction. So §6 should be read as a
change in the **idiom** of the documentary record, not as evidence that the state stopped applying
promotion pressure. The honest claim is about what documents* say*, and the natural follow-up is
whether the pressure moved into instruments that do not announce themselves in titles or bodies at
all — which this corpus, by construction, cannot see.*

## Limits

- **Co-occurrence is not attribution.** A document naming both 一票否决 and 安全生产 need not apply
  the veto to that target; it may discuss both. Every figure here is co-mention.
- **Small counts.** 96-278 documents per period, so a one-point move in a share is a handful of
  documents. Only the large swings (计划生育 40.6 → 0.0, 安全生产 30 → 10.7) carry weight.
- **2026 composition.** The corpus holds ~98k documents dated 2026 against ~29k for 2024, so the
  2023-26 column and the media share are inflated. The ordering is robust; the levels are not.
- **No fixed-site panel.** Every series on this corpus needs one (`rmb-coverage.md` §4 trap 3), and
  at 1,019 documents across many sites this one is too thin to build. Treat the periodisation as
  descriptive.
- **`admin_level_doc` is ~98% precise** (`corpus-lessons.md` A1), comfortably inside the swings used.

## Replication

```bash
python3 scripts/rnd/analysis/fts.py --cooccur-with 一票否决 \
    计划生育 安全生产 环境保护 生态环境 节能减排 食品安全 社会稳定 脱贫攻坚 耕地保护 粮食安全
```

The periodisation and the level table are a single pass joining those id sets to
`documents.date_published` and `doc_identity.admin_level_doc`. Route every term through
`fts.term_ids` rather than picking an index: 问责 and 债务 are two characters and need the segmented
index, while 一票否决 and 脱贫攻坚 need the trigram one.
