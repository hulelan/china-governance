# A bottom-up cascade, caught in full: 耐心资本 from a Beijing work-report line to the Third Plenum

*Findings memo, 2026-10-08. `bottom-up-channel.md` concluded that upward policy flow is nearly
invisible in the documentary record, and `corpus-lessons.md` B2 asked us to hunt it deliberately.
This is one case where the whole chain is visible and verifiable at the text level, including the
step the B2 memo predicted we would need and could not find: a central body **observing** local
practice before the center adopts it. The term is 耐心资本 ("patient capital"), it is a
state-finance instrument rather than the consumption or AI subjects the volume's other cascades
trace, and its central adoption is datable to a single Party document. Companion to
`bottom-up-channel.md`, `experimentation-wang-yang.md` and `successor-detector.md`.*

---

## The short version

The phrase appears in our corpus **first sub-nationally, on 2019-02-19, in a Beijing municipal
government work-report task assignment** — operative, with a named lead official and responsible
bureaus. It reaches a **central** document on **2023-07-11**, and the highest-level Party document
on **2024-07-21**. That is **five years and five months** from local operative use to the Third
Plenum's 《关于进一步全面深化改革 推进中国式现代化的决定》.

Between those two ends sit the two steps the experimentation literature says should exist and that
our corpus usually cannot see:

1. **Horizontal copying before central adoption.** Heilongjiang's 2022-08-12 provincial measures
   reproduce Beijing's June 2022 coinage near-verbatim — "培育一批'懂科技'的耐心资本。支持投资
   机构加大早期投资力度，激发早期投资活力" — two months after Beijing, and **eleven months before
   any central document in the corpus uses the term.**
2. **A central body observing localities.** The first central appearance is not a directive. It is
   an analytic piece by the **国家信息中心** (State Information Center, a central research
   institute) titled 《未来产业成为各地谋长远的重头戏》 — "future industries become the main event
   in localities' long-term planning" — arguing that this kind of industry "更需要有'耐心资本'支持".
   The center writes about the localities before it writes to them.

After the Third Plenum the term propagates in the ordinary top-down pattern, and fast: the monthly
volume goes from ~1-3 to 10 in 2024-07 and 47 by 2026-07.

## 2. Why this is a bottom-up case and not an artifact

Four ways it could be false, and what each check found.

- **A local document quoting a central text would match identically.** Checked by reading all
  seven earliest bodies. Every one is in its own operative register — a numbered task with an
  assigned bureau, a 工作原则, a subsidy rule. None quotes a central instrument.
- **Our Beijing coverage could simply be deeper than our central coverage for 2019-22.** It is
  not: the corpus holds ~26,100 `gov` and ~8,490 `bj` documents, and central sites are crawled
  back to 1999-2005. Central is over-sampled relative to Beijing, not under-sampled, so the
  four-year gap is not a sampling gap.
- **The 2023-07 central document could be mis-levelled.** `国家信息中心` is a central research
  institute under the NDRC and `admin_level_doc` calls it central, which is right. If anything
  this *understates* the finding: a research institute's analytic piece is weaker evidence of
  central adoption than the 2024-07 Party decision, and the Party decision is 5y5m after Beijing.
- **The term could be older in the world than in our corpus.** Almost certainly true, and it is
  the honest limit of the claim. 耐心资本 is a translation of a concept with its own history, and
  a leader's speech or a 人民日报 commentary outside our holdings could predate 2019. **The claim
  is about first operative use in the policy-document record we hold, not about coinage.**

## 3. What it does to the bottom-up finding

`bottom-up-channel.md` measured the upward channel as nearly invisible and quantified it on
citations: documents in the 典型经验 / 经验推广 / 示范 lexicon cite downward 0.31% of the time
against 0.24% for everything else — a real but tiny effect. This case says why that measurement is
a floor rather than a null:

**the upward channel here carries no citation at all.** Not one document in the chain cites
Beijing's 2019 work plan. The Third Plenum decision does not cite Heilongjiang, and the State
Information Center piece does not cite the 中关村 measures it is describing. What travels upward
is a **phrase**, and our citation machinery cannot see a phrase. The `localized_of` and
`title_reissue` channels cannot see it either, because no title is reused.

That has a direct methodological consequence for the volume. Upward flow measured on citations
will always look near-zero, not because it is absent but because **the upward channel's medium is
vocabulary, while the downward channel's medium is instruments.** A central body issues a document
that localities cite and re-issue; a locality coins a phrase that the center eventually writes into
a decision without attribution. The two directions are not symmetric objects, and measuring both
with one instrument guarantees the asymmetry we found.

**The testable version:** if this is general, then terms that end up in central Party documents
should show a detectable sub-national lead on average, with no citation trace. That is a tractable
study on this corpus — pick the operative noun phrases from the Third Plenum decision and the
14th/15th Five-Year-Plan recommendations, and date each one's first sub-national and first central
operative use. The present memo is n=1, chosen because it was clean; the design generalizes it.

## 4. Bearing on the NBER state-capital literature

Beraja, Peng, Yang and Yuchtman (**NBER w32701**, *Government as Venture Capitalists in AI*) study
government guidance funds as an innovation instrument from the fund and firm side. Our corpus holds
the half they do not: the **authorizing instruments**. Coverage is adequate for that — 1,769
documents mention 引导基金, 298 政府引导基金, 1,081 产业投资基金, 2,827 创业投资, and **267
documents whose title IS a fund instrument**, spanning 1998-2026 with a step from ~5/yr to ~18/yr
at 2016.

This memo is the lexical half of that replication and it adds something the fund-side data cannot
show: the **justificatory vocabulary** for long-horizon state capital was built sub-nationally, in
one city's technology-park finance reforms, and ratified centrally five years later. A fund-level
panel would date the instrument's spread; only the document record dates the *argument's* spread,
and here the argument leads.

### The missing half of the institution (measured 2026-10-09)

A bureaucracy that punishes losses cannot run a venture portfolio, so if patient capital is real
policy there should be a matching **blame-shield** for the officials deploying it. There is such a
vocabulary — `尽职免责` ("due diligence, exemption from liability") in **597** documents, `容错` in
1,093, `免责` in 1,050, `风险容忍` in 117 — and it is **older** than patient capital: 容错 runs
46-74 documents a year across 2018-2020 while 耐心资本 is 0-1 until 2022.

**But it was built for lending, not for funds.** Co-occurrence with the 597 `尽职免责` documents:

| context | share | | context | share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 融资 | **59%** | | 创业投资 | 28% |
| 银行 | **50%** | | 股权投资 | 24% |
| 贷款 | **49%** | | 引导基金 | 18% |
| 信贷 | 41% | | **耐心资本** | **9%** |

So the liability shield belongs to **credit officers making small-business loans** (小微企业 41%,
普惠金融 17%), and it reaches guidance funds only secondarily and patient capital barely. Within
documents, only **13%** of the 504 耐心资本 documents mention 容错 at all, while **23%** mention
引导基金 — patient capital is firmly tied to the *instrument* and loosely to the *protection*.

**The finding is therefore a gap, and it is falsifiable forward.** The state has asked for
long-horizon, loss-tolerant capital **without extending to fund managers the blame protection it
built for loan officers**. If patient capital is to function as policy rather than exhortation, a
fund-specific 免责 / 容错 regime should appear — in fund management measures, in 国资 performance
rules, or in the 政府投资基金 guidance. **If it does not appear within a few years, the better
reading is that 耐心资本 is a demand on private capital rather than a reform of state incentives.**
That is a prediction this corpus can check, and it costs nothing to re-run.

*(Caveat on the series: the 2026 counts are inflated by corpus composition — 2026 holds ~98k
documents against 2024's ~29k, the trap recorded in `rmb-coverage.md` §4 — so read the 2018-2020
versus 2022-2024 **ordering**, which is robust, not the 2026 levels.)*

## Limits

- **n=1.** One term, chosen because its chain is clean and short enough to read end to end. §3
  states the design that would generalize it; this memo does not run it.
- **"First in our corpus" is not "first in the world."** See §2. The 2019 Beijing document is the
  earliest *operative* use we hold, and the corpus does not contain most leadership speeches.
- **500 documents and 12 provinces** is the whole population here, so provincial comparisons
  inside it are thin. The 98 provincial / 102 municipal split is not a balanced panel.
- **Dates on two rows are malformed** in the source (`2022-06-16 21:042022-06-21 11:47` style,
  two stamps concatenated). They do not affect the ordering — both parse to June 2022 — but they
  are a reminder that `date_published` is text, per `corpus-lessons.md` A4.
- **The term is now saturated**, so its post-2024 series measures publicity rather than
  diffusion. Only the 2019-2023 segment carries the bottom-up signal.

## Replication

```bash
# the chain, by level
sqlite3 "file:documents.db?mode=ro" "
  SELECT i.admin_level_doc, COUNT(*), MIN(d.date_published)
  FROM documents d JOIN doc_identity i ON i.doc_id=d.id
  WHERE d.id IN (SELECT rowid FROM doc_search WHERE doc_search MATCH '\"耐心资本\"')
    AND d.date_published!='' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 3;"
```

Then **read the bodies** — the ordering alone does not distinguish a local coinage from a local
quotation of a central text, and that distinction is the entire finding. NOTE: query this through
`doc_search` (trigram), not `doc_search_seg`; jieba splits 耐心资本 into 耐心 + 资本 so the phrase
never matches as a token in the segmented index, and a 2-character term like 美元 fails in the
*trigram* index for the opposite reason (3-character minimum). Both failures return a clean zero.
