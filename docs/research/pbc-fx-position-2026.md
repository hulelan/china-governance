# The PBC's 2026-10-08 exchange-rate position, diffed against its own words thirteen days earlier

*Findings memo, 2026-10-08, written the day of publication. The People's Bank of China published
《中国人民银行关于人民币汇率的政策立场》 ("The PBC's Policy Position on the RMB Exchange Rate") at
16:23 on 2026-10-08 — a standalone, seven-section, ~6,100-character position statement, a document
type the bank almost never issues. This memo records what it says, and diffs it clause by clause
against the formulations the corpus shows were in use before it. The headline is an **omission**:
the canonical stability commitment that has appeared in 111 documents since 2008, and that the PBC's
own Monetary Policy Committee used **thirteen days earlier**, is absent. Companion to
`rmb-coverage.md` (what we hold on the RMB, and the three measurement traps).*

Source: `pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/2026100816231113295/index.html` (the node id is the
timestamp: 2026-10-08 16:23:11). Full text archived to the session scratchpad; **not yet in the
corpus** — see §5.

---

## 1. What the document is

Seven sections, opening with eight bulleted assertions and then arguing each:

1. 中国的汇率制度安排 — the regime: market supply and demand as the basis, adjusted with reference to
   a basket, managed float.
2. 人民币汇率水平的变化 — the level: +23% against the dollar since the July 2005 reform (8.27 → ~6.7),
   BIS nominal effective +50%, real effective +35%; ~+9% against the dollar since the start of 2025.
3. 中国没有必要，也无意通过汇率贬值获取贸易竞争优势 — never competitive devaluation.
4. 汇率与经常账户不存在简单线性关系 — no linear relation between the rate and the current account.
5. 国际上有关评估方法总体尚不成熟 — equilibrium-rate estimation methods are immature.
6. 缓解全球失衡需要逆差国和顺差国共同行动 — rebalancing is a shared responsibility.
7. 中国正积极推动经济增长模式转型 — China's own transition.

The audience is external. Section 5 is a direct, named methodological attack on the **IMF's External
Balance Assessment (EBA)**, arguing that treating its output as the "official basis" (官方依据) for
calling the RMB undervalued is "a misreading and misuse" (曲解和误用) — that EBA is an
external-imbalance diagnostic rather than an equilibrium-rate model, that its three modules
disagree "even in direction", and that it assesses the **real effective** rate and so cannot be
read onto the nominal, still less onto the bilateral dollar rate.

## 3. The omission, with a thirteen-day baseline in the same institution's voice

`保持人民币汇率在合理均衡水平上的基本稳定` — "keep the RMB exchange rate basically stable at a
reasonable, equilibrium level" — has been the standing commitment. It appears in **111 documents we
hold, from 2008-12 to 2026-09**, and it was **not retired**: it was in use ten to thirteen days
before this statement, including by the PBC itself.

The decisive comparison is the PBC's **Monetary Policy Committee Q3 2026 readout, 2026-09-25**:

> 坚持市场在汇率形成中的决定性作用，增强外汇市场韧性，稳定市场预期，同时防范市场"羊群效应"和
> 非理性预期的自我强化，**保持人民币汇率在合理均衡水平上的基本稳定**。

The 2026-10-08 position statement **keeps the first three clauses** — the decisive role of the
market, and almost verbatim the 羊群效应 / 非理性预期的自我强化 justification for intervention —
and **drops the last one.** The two halves travelled together in the bank's own voice on 09-25; on
10-08 only one survives. A 2026-09-28 CPPCC piece by 魏革军, a PBC counsellor (中国人民银行参事),
pairs them the same way.

So this is **not** a vocabulary change working through the system. It is a choice specific to a
document whose entire subject is the exchange rate.

**What the substitution does.** The old clause is a *commitment about an outcome* ("we will keep it
stable around equilibrium") and it presupposes that an equilibrium level is identifiable. The new
framing is a *disclaimer about intent*: the market is decisive, no target level is preset, no
long-term trend is managed, routine intervention ended in 2017. Section 5 then argues that
equilibrium levels **cannot be reliably estimated at all**. The two positions are in tension: a
commitment to stability "at a reasonable equilibrium level" is awkward to hold alongside an argument
that equilibrium-rate estimation is immature and its conclusions unusable. Dropping the clause
removes that tension — and removes a commitment that an external party could measure compliance
against.

## 4. Three genuinely new formulations

- **`退出常态化外汇干预`** — "after 2017 the PBC exited routine foreign-exchange intervention."
  **Zero prior occurrences** in a corpus holding 9,543 documents that mention 人民币. This dates the
  end of routine intervention to a specific year in a formal statement.
- **`外部平衡评估` / EBA by name** — **zero prior occurrences.** The corpus has never seen the PBC
  engage the IMF's assessment framework by name, let alone argue against its construction.
- **`自2027年起将进一步向IMF报送外汇相关数据`** — a forward data-transparency commitment, offered
  in the same breath as the methodological attack. The document pairs a concession on transparency
  with a refusal on substance.

And one unusually candid admission rather than a new phrase: the statement says the bank uses
macroprudential tools and **"乃至在极端情景下直接进行外汇干预"** — "and in extreme scenarios even
intervenes directly in the foreign-exchange market" — naming the pandemic and **the April 2025
tariff war** as occasions. A statement arguing that the market is decisive also concedes direct
intervention, bounded to short-term disorder rather than the trend, and immediately cites 2008
emerging-market precedent and **the July 2026 joint intervention when the yen hit a near-40-year
low** as comparators.

## 5. What this says about our coverage, and one gap it exposes

`rmb-coverage.md` found RMB internationalization reaching the documentary record as **zone-and-plan
policy rather than monetary regulation** — the most-cited carriers being 大湾区纲要, the Five-Year
Plan recommendations, 深圳先行示范区 — with PBC and SAFE not in the top fourteen sites. This document
**confirms that split from the other side**: it is 6,100 characters on the exchange rate from the
central bank itself, and it does not contain 人民币国际化, 跨境人民币 or 数字人民币 once, though all
three are active elsewhere (237 / 587 / 469 documents). The bank defends the *rate*; the zones and
plans promote the *currency*.

**A crawler gap this exposed.** The document sits in `goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469` (新闻/沟通交流),
and `crawlers/pbc.py` covers only the two 条法司 document sections (规范性文件 and 部门规章). The
pagination fix of 2026-10-08 (`af874c7`) takes PBC from 31 to ~541 documents but **would not have
caught this statement.** A position statement of this weight living outside the regulation sections
is an argument for adding that section — and note that the two cross-posted rows the pager audit
found pointing into `goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469` were pointing at exactly this subsection. Logged
in CLAUDE.md's Open Questions.

## Limits

- **Written the day of publication**, from the single primary source. No subsequent clarification,
  English version, or commentary is reflected, and the bank may issue any of those.
- **"Zero prior occurrences" means zero in our corpus**, which holds 31 PBC documents (all since
  2025-12, until the pagination fix lands) and no 货币政策执行报告 series. A phrase could be
  established in PBC publications we do not hold. The 合理均衡水平 finding does **not** have this
  weakness — it rests on the phrase's *presence* in 111 documents including the PBC's own MPC
  readout, which is a positive observation.
- **An omission is not a policy change.** It is evidence about a document. The stronger reading —
  that the commitment has been withdrawn — would need the phrase to stop appearing in subsequent
  PBC output, which is a measurement to repeat in a quarter, not a conclusion available today.
- The level figures (+23% bilateral, +50% NEER, +35% REER, +9% in 2025) are the bank's own and are
  not independently checked here.
