Read the denominators. Each cell is documents of that speaker type using the term per 1,000 of that speaker type's documents that week, counting one copy per title per speaker type per week (a wire story reposted by forty portals counts once). The media layer before 2025 is crawl-thin (state media, commentary and business press are almost entirely 2025–26 in the corpus), so a long media series measures our crawl, not the discourse; read across speakers within a week, not down a media column across years. Diplomacy and research are thin every week. Counts are regex matches on title + body, so a term inside a fixed formula counts like a live use. Method and caveats: global-discourse-map.
